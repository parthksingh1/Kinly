# Demo script

**Setup before recording:** both servers running. A tablet or second window shows `/screen/kamala-demo-screen` (press **Talk to Kinly** once, then **Done talking**, so audio is unlocked). A laptop shows Arjun's dashboard and `/demo`. Your phone number is in `/demo` for the Bolna call. Press **Reset demo** first.
Backup clips: put `checkin.mp4`, `phone.mp4`, `fall.mp4`, `scam.mp4`, `missed_medicine.mp4` or `video.mp4` in `family-app/public/demo-clips/`. A **Backup clip** button then appears on that step.

## 60-second pitch

"My grandmother lives alone. Every day she waits for a phone call, and we're always too busy. Kinly calls her every morning, in Hindi, on the phone she already has. It remembers that her knee hurt yesterday and that her roses are blooming. It reminds her about her blood pressure tablet. If she mentions a fall, chest pain, or a stranger asking for her OTP, Kinly tells her exactly what to do and alerts our whole family on WhatsApp within seconds. We see a two-line summary every day and a suggestion of what to talk about when we call. She can say 'call Riya' and a video call starts. Kinly runs on Bolna for real phone calls, Sarvam and Deepgram for every Indian and global language, and it's built on a 14,000-star open source companion we rebuilt for the people who need companionship most. It's consent-first, always honest that it's an AI, and it never asks for money. We're launching in India and the US at $19 a month, which is less than one weekly coffee for a call every single day."
